# Future organization extensions

The v0.1 operating scope is Product & Engineering. The Director remains a generic root so future functions can be added without putting them under the CTO.

Within the current scope, `product-engineering-operating-model.md` defines six durable domains. Those domains do not require six managers or six additional agents. The current ten-agent organization covers them through explicit accountability, responsible specialists, independent review, and a visible Data Platform & AI Quality staffing gap.

Expected extension:

```text
Director of Optiak
├── Product and Experience
├── Engineering Assurance
├── Security and AI Governance
└── Finance and Operations
    ├── Finance and Business Operations
    └── Infrastructure FinOps
```

Finance agents should not be created until billing, cloud, provider usage, revenue, and accounting sources are available with explicit metric contracts. They begin read-only and may never execute payments, alter budgets, or change infrastructure without Board approval.

The same evidence-based creation gates apply to every future function: recurring work, stable sources, bounded outputs, distinct authority or permissions, explicit handoffs and review, fixture validation, paused import, and a measured bottleneck or control risk.
