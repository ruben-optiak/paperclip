The frozen export shows a near-total coupling between visibility and stock: 1,339 `publish|instock`, 301 `private|outofstock`, five `private|onbackorder`, one `publish|outofstock` and one `private|instock`. This may hide discontinued or temporarily unavailable pages and can affect SEO, links and Merchant coverage, but the export alone cannot prove the intended policy.

Recover the configured WooCommerce, theme, feed and operational rules that control product status. Classify private products as discontinued, temporary stock gaps, incomplete drafts, duplicates or unknown. Measure organic and feed impact before proposing any status change. Never publish a private product simply because stock is available, and never privatize a product solely because it is out of stock without an approved policy.

Acceptance requires a documented policy, an exact exception list, redirect/canonical handling for discontinued products, rollback rules and a Board-reviewed changeset. No visibility or stock mutation is authorized by this task.
