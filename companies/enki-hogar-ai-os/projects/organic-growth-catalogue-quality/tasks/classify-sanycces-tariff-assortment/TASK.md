The official 2026 national tariff contains 7,660 unique references. The current
published catalogue exposes 1,196 exact tariff SKUs and uses two additional tariff
references as components of 21 Cardiff composite variants. The remaining 6,462
tariff references are not represented by a published commercial SKU.

Do not treat this difference as 6,462 missing products. The tariff mixes sellable
products, finishes, dimensions, replacement parts, accessories, services and
machining operations across catalogue families that have not all been supplied or
reviewed. Classify the rows by product family and entity role, bind each group to
the applicable official catalogue evidence, then compare the result with Enki's
assortment decision and current Woo identities.

Acceptance requires a complete disposition of the 6,462 rows into sellable
candidate, component, service, duplicate/alias, superseded or unresolved; an exact
catalogue and page reference for each sellable family; preserved official PVP and
EAN evidence; collision checks against all published and private Woo SKUs; and a
Board-approved shortlist. This task grants no authority to create drafts, change
prices or publish products.
