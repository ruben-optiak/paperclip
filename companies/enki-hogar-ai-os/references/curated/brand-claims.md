# Enki Hogar brand and claims contract

Snapshot: 2026-08-29. Curated from `docs/empresa.md` and `docs/producto-crm.md`. It is a conservative drafting contract, not a complete brand book and not live commercial data.

## Confirmed

- Enki Hogar is a Spanish ecommerce business focused on bathroom and kitchen products.
- Its documented positioning emphasizes design, quality, value, and practical information that helps a customer choose.
- Customer-facing copy should be clear, useful and specific. Product facts must preserve manufacturer evidence and distinguish confirmed compatibility from guidance.
- Current price, stock, availability, delivery, promotion and product status require an approved live source and a visible observation date.

## Evidence required

- Dimensions, materials, finishes, installation, compatibility, warranty and technical performance require authoritative product evidence.
- Delivery dates, stock, price, discount and availability require a current approved commerce or logistics source.
- Comparative or superlative claims such as “best”, “cheapest”, “fastest”, “premium” or “guaranteed” require explicit, current evidence and human approval.
- Legal, returns, refund and warranty wording requires an approved current policy; historical notes are not sufficient.

## Unknown — do not invent

- Official tagline, formal brand voice guide and approved vocabulary.
- Default treatment of `tú` versus `usted` across channels.
- Rules for humor, emojis, urgency, scarcity and promotional pressure.
- Approved delivery, price-match, sustainability, exclusivity or satisfaction guarantees.
- Channel-specific legal disclaimers and service-level promises.

An `UNKNOWN` is not a prohibition on future policy. Until the Board confirms it, omit the claim or present a neutral alternative and request a decision.

## V1 boundaries

- Never present a supplier, historical snapshot, inference or draft as current Enki policy.
- Never expose PII, credentials or internal commercial notes in customer-facing copy.
- Every reviewed output remains `BORRADOR — NO PUBLICADO/NO ENVIADO`; Brand Guardian does not grant authority to publish or contact a customer.
