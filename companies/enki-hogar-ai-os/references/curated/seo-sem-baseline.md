# SEO and SEM baseline

Snapshot: 2026-08-29. Historical context; query live systems for current metrics.

SEO analysis should cover technical discoverability, indexation signals, category and product taxonomy, content intent, catalogue completeness, and brand/category demand. GSC normally has reporting delay, so briefs must show the last available date.

Paid acquisition analysis should keep platform-attributed conversion value separate from WooCommerce sales and from accounting margin. Shopping-related readiness and Merchant Center suspension recovery are initial priorities. Campaign containment or prioritization may be recommended, but campaign, bid, budget, audience, feed, and conversion mutations are blocked.
