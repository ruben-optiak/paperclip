# Tracking baseline

Snapshot: 2026-08-29. Historical context; verify all current behavior.

Tracking has known operational history and should begin in a yellow state until proven by source health, event definitions, attribution notes, and reconciliation. The Technology Manager owns diagnosis; Growth and Finance own interpretation within their domains.

Every material metric should state source, period, freshness, timezone, currency where applicable, and whether it is observed, calculated, or inferred. Connector failure, partial periods, lagging GSC data, and attribution differences must remain visible.
