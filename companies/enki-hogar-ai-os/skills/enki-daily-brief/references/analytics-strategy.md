# Analytics strategy and backlog

Snapshot: 2026-08-29. Baseline and priorities must be revalidated with live sources.

The operating goal is profitable, measurable growth rather than channel-local optimization. The initial priority chain is:

1. Prove connector and tracking health.
2. Reconcile definitions and attribution differences between WooCommerce, GA4, GSC, and Google Ads.
3. Resolve Merchant Center readiness as a high-priority governed workstream.
4. Prioritize catalogue and organic opportunities with evidence.
5. Evaluate paid acquisition only with explicit cost and margin limitations.

Measurement quality was previously treated as incomplete rather than fully trusted. No historical note should be interpreted as proof that a current configuration is healthy.
