# Enki Hogar context

Snapshot: 2026-08-29. Historical context, not live operational data.

Enki Hogar is a Spanish ecommerce business focused on bathroom and kitchen products. Its working proposition emphasizes design, quality, value, and the practical information a customer needs to choose compatible products.

The initial AI OS should improve decision quality across catalogue, acquisition, measurement, and customer care while leaving commercial and operational control with the user. WooCommerce is the commerce system of record for the v1 read path. Any current sales, stock, order, or product statement must be obtained through that connector and dated.

No personal customer information, credentials, private access routes, or bulk exports are included in this snapshot.
