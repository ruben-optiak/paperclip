Enki Hogar AI OS is a six-agent operating team for the Enki Hogar ecommerce business. Ecommerce owns catalogue and Merchant readiness, Growth owns acquisition, SEO and editorial execution, and the other specialists retain clear finance, technology, and customer-experience boundaries. Version 0.15.0 adds a bounded public SEO pipeline, actual provider-read authentication probes, source-specific period contracts and replayable technical/search priorities. Its dated baseline covers 788 inventory URLs and 60 sampled pages; restored GSC/GA4 reads add 35 search review opportunities, 21 partial overlap candidates and 19 index observations. Exact-filter recovery handles truncated GSC URL cells without guessing identity. Missing canonical fields, rounded positions and omitted query cells remain explicit limitations. Existing measurement, finance, WordPress and Web Vitals contracts retain their conservative semantics. The EAI-022 catalogue-evidence boundary still admits only exact Board-approved runs and field revisions into a hashed closed projection. WooCommerce live remains the only current commercial catalogue. Pack import, Woo import, reindex, publication, optimization and live configuration remain operator-only.

The audited Telegram edge, editorial memory and seven-stage workflow keep the conservative operating boundary. Research and shortlist share a fingerprint; Ecommerce validates the exact set; Board decides on that revision; and a newer brief must apply the decision before drafting. Feedback remains an observation, results use closed 7/28/90-day windows, and only Board may promote a lesson into a tested versioned rule. Agents may read approved sources, analyse data and prepare local drafts. Only Growth can request one of three governed publication calls—WordPress post upsert, Facebook Page post or Instagram image—and each request needs the exact reviewed revision, a stable idempotency key, Paperclip approval from Board and the connector's independent write switch. All other publishing operations, customer messages, campaigns, prices, stock, orders, budgets, infrastructure and support-storage mutations remain blocked.

The Director de Operaciones is the single organizational root. All five specialists report directly to the Director in a hub-and-spoke organization; there is no Chief of Staff layer. Paperclip represents the root internally with its CEO-compatible role, but this does not confer Board authority or permission to bypass an approval.
